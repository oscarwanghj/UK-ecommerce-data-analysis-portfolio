    Method and data notes

    Data: UCI Online Retail dataset, a real UK online gift retailer, Dec 2010 – Dec 2011 (541,909 raw lines). Analysis in Python (pandas), with code drafted by Claude and validated by me. Customers were scored on recency, frequency (number of orders) and spend, then grouped into six segments. £ impacts are illustrative estimates, not forecasts.
